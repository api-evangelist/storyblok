A migration guide for moving from @storyblok/richtext v4 to v5, whose new rendering architecture focuses on smaller bundles, framework-first rendering, and a simplified public API. It covers breaking changes such as the removal of richTextResolver, updates to the React, Vue, Astro, and Svelte SDKs, and new utilities for image optimization and table rendering.
