Enterprise asset management typically fails because organizations treat assets as property-level problems rather than organization-wide solutions, resulting in duplicated files and inconsistent governance. The article advocates for centralizing asset management within a CMS to eliminate this fragmentation. Storyblok's Enterprise Assets Library addresses these challenges by providing shared libraries, organization-level governance, and integrated asset access across multiple teams and regions.
